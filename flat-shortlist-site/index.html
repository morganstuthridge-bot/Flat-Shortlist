How the scoring works

Trade Me and realestate.co.nz are scanned for Christchurch rentals. A listing is dropped if rent is over $700, it has fewer than 2 bedrooms, it is on a major road, it is available before 1 December, or the drive is too long (target 30 minutes for Mel and Roisin, 40 for Morgan, with 5 minutes of tolerance).

The rest are scored out of 100 on rent, a second bathroom (one-bathroom places lose 18 points), dwelling type, garage, storage, off-street parking, quiet street, how modern it reads, heating and glazing, sun and outdoor space, drive time, suburb and extras. Orange flags are things to check before a viewing. Drive times are estimates to within about five minutes.
